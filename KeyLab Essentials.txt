Arturia KeyLab Essentials

This device definition provides: 
- 61-key keyboard support (via standard MIDI Note messages)
- 9 knobs (knob1..knob9)
- 9 faders (fader1..fader9)
- 8 velocity-sensitive pads (pad[0] .. pad[7]) with LED feedback
- Transport and context buttons

Notes:
- To enable the skin background, place your device image into `skin/images/DeviceBackground.png` (recommended name: KeyLab Essentials.png or DeviceBackground.png).
- The device supports visual feedback and display updates (set in .device file).
